About corner handling

Overshoot / Return: native SVG geometry remains the master path. High-resolution analysis detects sharp rendered corners and maps them back to exact native joins before applying the G1 recovery move.

Native-segment model: line corners come directly from SVG L/H/V geometry; curves use exact endpoint derivatives. No sampled-point corner reconstruction is used.
